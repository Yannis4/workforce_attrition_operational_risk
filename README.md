# Workforce Attrition Analytics for Operational Risk

## Business Problem
Operational performance in logistics and supply chain environments is
highly dependent on skilled labor availability. High attrition among
key roles (drivers, planners, warehouse staff) introduces operational
risk, capacity loss, and service disruption.

## Objective
To analyze workforce attrition drivers and assess how people-related
factors can signal operational risk before performance deteriorates.

## Dataset
Synthetic HR dataset representing workforce demographics, tenure,
performance indicators, and attrition outcomes.

## Methodology
- Exploratory data analysis to identify attrition patterns
- Feature engineering on tenure, compensation, and engagement indicators
- Classification modeling to identify high-risk employee segments

## Key Insights
- Attrition risk is strongly linked to tenure volatility and job role
  characteristics
- Certain workforce segments present early warning signals of capacity risk
- Analytics can support proactive retention and workforce planning decisions

## Business Impact
This approach can help operations leaders:
- Anticipate capacity gaps
- Reduce unplanned downtime
- Support workforce planning in critical supply chain roles

## Tools
Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn, Matplotlib, Seaborn

## Limitations & Next Steps
- Synthetic dataset
- Future integration with operational KPIs (OTIF, productivity)
